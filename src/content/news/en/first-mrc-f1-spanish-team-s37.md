Spain has owned a Formula 1 team since 2018. However, **it still raced under the Italian flag,** as it had shareholders of other nationalities.

**This will change from Season 37**, when the team will fly the Spanish flag and the shareholders or owners will all be of the same nationality, joined by jeansapia, a Brazilian manager who is expected to become assistant to the team principal (nicorz).

**The team’s new identity is currently being put to a vote**, according to sources within the team, and will be unveiled in the coming weeks, featuring a new name, a new car and new drivers. For the moment, the only thing known is that one of the drivers will be the current number one, Marc **Vilanova**. There are still doubts regarding the second seat.
