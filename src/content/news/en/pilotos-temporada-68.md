Hello Pegaso fans,
It’s time for our seasonal update on your driver’s progress. Is your driver a winner, did he gain a few spots, or is he just taking a different path?

Over the past seasons, we’ve seen a lot of Pegaso drivers get a seat in F1. Whether they were able to do that with a top, mid or low-level team will make a difference for these drivers’ future. But their managers will have gained experience for seasons to come, both in driving F1 and in building driver careers, realising that it’s possible to get to the top series in MRC.

The academy of fans and friends keeps delivering. Congratulations to **_Juan Barnard_**, **_Nacer Gonza_** and **_Madalynn Clara_** and their respective managers. All these drivers will make their F1 debut next season. And we are still counting. As we speak, there are quite a few open seats left for next season.
